# Alt-Managers — 2026-W46 (window: ~2026-10-01 to 2026-11-09)

## What moved
Activity among the key alt-manager watchlist (SoftBank, Blackstone, BlackRock/GIP,
DigitalBridge, Brookfield, Apollo, Goldman Sachs; KKR, Blue Owl on watch) was thin
this specific window relative to the summer's mega-announcements. Two disclosed,
real capital-allocation events landed inside the window, plus one vague regional
pledge filed as a candidate:

1. **Blackstone — $8.7B final close, Blackstone Energy Transition Partners V**
   (2026-10-19, `fund_launch`, verified_alpha). Hard-capped, >50% bigger than its
   predecessor. Strategy is control equity in power/energy businesses; the fund's
   named platforms and acquisitions (Flow Control Holdings' liquid-cooling
   components, Legence, Sabre Industries, plus gas-fired power projects) sit
   squarely in the data-center power-demand story even though the release itself
   doesn't use the word "AI." This is this class's bread and butter: the
   buildout's balance sheet, one tranche at a time.

2. **Apollo — minority equity stake in Mubadala Capital** (announced 2026-10-26,
   `minority_stake`, verified via Apollo's own IR release). Apollo plus a set of
   undisclosed strategic investors are putting ~$5B in aggregate into Mubadala
   Investment Company's $60B+ alternative-asset-management arm at initial close;
   Apollo's own slice is not disclosed (Reuters sources, uncorroborated by Apollo,
   put it near 10%). Expected to close Q4 2026. Structurally this isn't an AI deal,
   but it deepens Apollo's ties into the Mubadala/MGX sovereign network that is
   itself one of the most active AI-infra sovereign allocators — flagged as a
   `discovered_allocators` candidate (Mubadala Capital as its own GP-level node).

3. **Brookfield — "tens of billions of euros" in Germany over 5 years**
   (announced 2026-10-19 ahead of the Invest in Germany summit, `candidate`).
   Brookfield's own release names energy, digital infrastructure and industrial
   as the targets and explicitly cites AI/digitalization demand, but gives no
   specific vehicle, project or firm number — filed as a candidate per SCOPE
   rules (vague target/program size, not a committed allocation) even though the
   source is Tier-1. Germany's Economy Ministry separately tallied >$252B of
   *all* corporate pledges at the same summit; don't conflate that bloc total
   with Brookfield's unspecified share.

## What didn't move (checked, no new in-window event)
- **SoftBank**: the $10B third/final tranche into OpenAI (completing the $30B
  Vision Fund 2 program) disclosed 2026-10-01 is *already on file*
  (`engine.edgar exists` confirms id 369, verified) — not re-filed. No other
  SoftBank capital-allocation disclosure found in the window.
- **BlackRock/GIP**: Q3 earnings (filed 2026-10-14) was results/IR-conference
  only — no new deal. The Aligned Data Centers $40B close and the Meta/El Paso
  $12.5B debt financing both predate the window (July 2026) and aren't re-filed.
- **DigitalBridge**: still a SoftBank subsidiary as of its Sept 30 merger close
  (one day pre-window); its pending ArcLight acquisition (up to $1.05B) remains
  un-closed per the most recent 10-Q — no confirmation found that it closed in
  this window.
- **Goldman Sachs, KKR, Blue Owl**: 8-Ks checked were earnings, board/governance,
  debt facility amendments, or an IR presentation — none a capital-allocation
  event. No new press-side deal found for any of the three in the window either.

## Weak lead flagged, not dropped
NVIDIA's six-firm ($500B target) AI-compute financing platform MOUs — signed
2026-08-10 with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR
— remain MOUs. No report found of any platform converting to a definitive,
capital-committed agreement as of 2026-11-09. Not filed as a row (pre-window,
and still non-binding), but worth a standing watch: any one of the six signing
a definitive agreement would be this class's biggest event of the quarter.
