# Sovereigns — 2026-W46 summary

## What moved
Thin week for the core three Gulf funds (MGX, Mubadala, Saudi PIF) — nothing closed
in the ~Oct 14-Nov 9 window beyond talk-stage leads. US Government (tier:watch) was
the active allocator this week, almost entirely through Commerce's CHIPS R&D program
and the Pentagon's Office of Strategic Capital.

## Biggest signals
1. **Commerce Dept CHIPS R&D letters of intent, $389M across 4 companies (2026-10-22).**
   Helion ($175M), Commonwealth Fusion Systems ($85M), Vertical Semiconductor ($73M)
   and Sibelco ($56M) each get a non-voting minority equity stake rider on their CHIPS
   R&D award — the same structure the engine already has on file for GlobalFoundries'
   July 2026 batch. Fusion (Helion, CFS) and the AI-compute power-semiconductor supply
   chain (Vertical Semiconductor's GaN devices, Sibelco's ultra-high-purity quartz)
   are both now explicit US sovereign-equity targets, not just grant recipients.
2. **GlobalFoundries equity stake widened again, tied to the $21B US-Japan Malta/NY
   fab deal (2026-10-14).** The $955M CHIPS reallocation half of this announcement was
   already on file (id 442); the new piece is a further ~4% Commerce stake, reported
   as raising the government's total from 3% to 7% — but sources disagree on the
   baseline and no dollar value was disclosed, so it's filed as a candidate pending a
   cleaner primary figure.
3. **Neros gets a $450M conditional OSC loan (~2026-11-02)** for domestic drone
   manufacturing — the third such drone-sector OSC commitment this year (after PDW's
   $820M in July) — plus a disputed, unsized non-voting equity stake that OSC itself
   has not confirmed.
