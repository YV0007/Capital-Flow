# VC Allocators — ISO Week 2026-W33
_Disclosures ~2026-07-15 to 2026-08-07. 7 verified rows / 1 candidate row across 4 of 7 watchlist firms._

## What moved

A quieter window than last week's clean sweep, but three distinct threads: watchlist
VCs backing the **physical inputs to the AI buildout** one layer further upstream
(critical-minerals mining, APAC GPU-cloud capacity, defense-manufacturing hardware),
plus one large unconfirmed number about the biggest single position in the group's
history. Khosla, a16z, Coatue, Sequoia and Founders Fund all produced attributable
rows; Thrive Capital and Altimeter did not clear the bar this window (see below).

## Biggest signals

1. **Senra Systems $65M Series B — three watchlist VCs in one round.** Sequoia,
   a16z and Founders Fund all participated (Lowercarbon Capital and Interlagos
   co-led) in an ex-SpaceX-founded company modernizing wire-harness manufacturing
   for defense, maritime and space-launch customers. Same pattern as last week's
   Hadrian: watchlist capital backing the unglamorous physical-manufacturing layer
   of defense-tech, not another model wrapper. Company PR (2026-07-15) doesn't say
   whether the three VCs are new or returning investors.

2. **Mariana Minerals $310M Series B — Khosla leads, a16z returns.** AI/robotics-run
   copper and lithium mining and refining (MarianaOS stack), at a $1.5B valuation.
   Off the core AI-compute taxonomy but mapped to robotics/embodied-AI given the
   "AI-powered robots" framing of the operation - and critical minerals feed the
   same battery/chip supply chains the rest of the universe cares about.
   Washington Harbour Partners (flagged as a discovered allocator last week too)
   and Breakthrough Energy Ventures also rode alongside.

3. **Coatue's Firmus follow-on — $2B at a $10.5B post ($5.5B in April).** Nvidia's
   APAC AI-factory partner in Australia raised a fully-subscribed $2B round; Coatue
   and Nvidia are framed by Firmus's own PR as "follow-on participation," with
   Blackstone and Jane Street supplying the new capital. Some press headlines
   loosely call it "led by Coatue, Nvidia" — graded participant here on the
   company's more precise language, with the discrepancy noted in-row.

4. **Sequoia's reported $10B Anthropic stake — real number, wrong shape for an
   event.** A Bloomberg profile of Sequoia's new co-stewards (Alfred Lin, Pat
   Grady) put a first-ever number, ~$10B, on Sequoia's cumulative Anthropic
   position — the largest single investment in the firm's 54-year history. It is
   not a new priced transaction; it appears to describe how Sequoia's January
   entry and May Series H co-lead role accumulated. Every outlet repeating the
   figure traces to the single Aug-5 Bloomberg exclusive, so it stays `candidate`
   despite being the single largest number in this run.
