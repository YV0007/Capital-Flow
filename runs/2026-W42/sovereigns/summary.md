# Sovereigns — 2026-W42 summary

## What moved
The clearest confirmed money this cycle came from **US Government / CHIPS Act**, not
from MGX, Mubadala or PIF directly. On 2026-09-08 the Commerce Department finalized a
batch of CHIPS-and-Science-Act R&D grants converting May-2026 letters of intent into
definitive agreements: **GlobalFoundries ($375M)**, **D-Wave Quantum ($100M)**,
**Rigetti Computing ($100M)**, **PsiQuantum ($100M)**, and **Quantinuum ($100M)** — all
for quantum-wafer/quantum-computing R&D. A week later (2026-09-16) the largest single
award finalized: **$1B to Anderon**, a newly spun-off IBM subsidiary running a 300mm
pure-play quantum wafer foundry in Albany, NY, matched by $1B cash from IBM. Several of
these awards give Commerce a minority, non-controlling equity stake as a funding
condition — a recurring structural feature of this program worth tracking as its own
pattern. All six rows are `verified` off primary nist.gov pages.

## Biggest signals
1. **CHIPS quantum-foundry push is a real, disclosed capital program**, not just
   letters of intent — $1.775B finalized across 6 awards in a five-week span.
2. **Gulf sovereigns went quiet on NEW committed capital this cycle.** MGX's $49B
   Fund I close and its Anthropic/OpenAI/xAI stakes are all pre-window (Jul/Feb 2026).
   Mubadala's only Sept activity was an AI-infrastructure *exploration* MOU with
   Together AI (Sept 29) — explicitly no capital disclosed, so not filed as an event.
3. **Two live Gulf mega-leads remain unresolved and are filed as candidates**:
   Mubadala/MGX's reported ¥1–2tn ($6.3–12.6B) Akita, Japan data-center talks (still
   "no final decision" per Mubadala as of the latest reporting found), and PIF's
   HUMAIN seeking to raise a $2.5B Saudi data-center fund (pending CMA approval,
   unconfirmed by HUMAIN).
