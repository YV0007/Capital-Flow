# VC Agent — Weekly Summary — 2026-W35

## Headline
Late-August 2026 was a heavy week for the watchlist: 23 verified/verified_alpha events
across all seven tracked VCs, spanning nuclear, defense-tech, AI-data infrastructure,
neocloud, cybersecurity, and AI-applications. Three deals in particular pulled in
multiple watchlist names at once (Databricks, Volta, Castelion), and one weak signal
(Khosla Ventures' reported $5.5B fund) is logged as a candidate pending a close.

## Biggest signals
1. **Databricks' $5B Series L at a $190B valuation (Aug 13)** drew Coatue as lead,
   with Andreessen Horowitz and Thrive Capital both back as existing-investor
   participants alongside Blackstone, MGX, T. Rowe Price and new investor Sixth
   Street Growth — a rare single round touching three watchlist firms plus two
   already-tracked alt-managers (Blackstone, MGX).
2. **AI-infrastructure capital continues to scale past $1B checks**: Sequoia led
   Valar Atomics' $1B nuclear Series B (Aug 3, $6B valuation), and Andreessen
   Horowitz co-led Castelion's $1B hypersonics Series C (Aug 19, $13B valuation)
   alongside JPMorgan's Strategic Investment Group and Carlyle — with Altimeter
   also participating in the latter. Defense-tech and energy-for-AI are both
   showing sustained, not one-off, watchlist conviction.
3. **Neocloud/GPU-capacity is a fresh convergence point**: Andreessen Horowitz and
   Altimeter co-led Volta AI Infra Holdings' $300M raise (Aug 4, $2.4B valuation,
   tied to a $10B/6yr cloud contract), while Coatue backed Firmus's $2B APAC
   AI-datacenter raise (Aug 7) alongside NVIDIA and new investor Blackstone
   Tactical Opportunities — two different watchlist firms backing two different
   AI-cloud landlords in the same ten-day span.

## Notable smaller moves
- Sequoia led Corma's $60M cybersecurity-AI seed (Aug 10) with Khosla Ventures and
  Coatue both participating in the same round — three watchlist firms, one deal.
- Khosla Ventures co-led Veeda AI's $90M world-models seed (Aug 19) with Radical
  Ventures.
- Thrive Capital disclosed a new $215.5M Amazon public-equity stake via its Q2 2026
  13F (filed/disclosed Aug 14) and backed Ambrook's $30M fintech-AI Series B (Aug 6).
- Founders Fund was comparatively quiet this week — its one confirmed new capital
  event was a $50M participation in Network Bio's biomedical-AI launch financing
  (Aug 19); a Founders Fund-attributed Series B in Khartis Therapeutics could NOT be
  corroborated (the round was actually led by Forge Life Science Partners with no
  Founders Fund mention) and was dropped rather than filed.
- Etched's $700M raise (Aug 18, led by Jane Street) and Rillet's $100M Series C
  (Aug 19, led by ICONIQ) each pulled in both Sequoia and Andreessen Horowitz as
  participants — filed as separate rows per allocator per the sector_swarm counting
  rule.
