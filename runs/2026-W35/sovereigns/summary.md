# Sovereigns — 2026-W35 (week of 2026-08-24)

## What moved
15 verified events, 1 candidate. All four watchlist entities (MGX, Mubadala, Saudi PIF, US Government)
were active in the trailing ~30 days.

1. **Saudi PIF closed the $55B Electronic Arts buyout (Aug 4)** — the largest LBO in history. PIF rolled its
   existing EA stake into ~$36B of consortium equity (vs. $20B JPMorgan debt) and now holds 93.4% of a
   private EA, alongside Silver Lake (5.5%) and Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners (1.1%). Filed under
   `diversified-pe` per the canonical sector note — a gaming/entertainment take-private, not AI buildout,
   but real, enormous committed capital.

2. **MGX had two AI-infrastructure moves**: it co-invested in Databricks' $5B strategic round (Aug 13,
   Coatue-led, $190B valuation) alongside Blackstone/T. Rowe Price/Sixth Street, and — together with
   BlackRock's GIP under the AIP banner — committed a further $5B of growth capital to Aligned Data
   Centers (Jul 21) immediately after closing their $40B take-private of the developer. MGX's individual
   slice was not disclosed in either deal (both filed with amount_usd blank, round_total_usd populated).

3. **Mubadala led Moove's $250M Series C** (Aug 5, autonomous-mobility infrastructure, $2.1B valuation,
   co-leads Woven Capital/Toyota and Ion Pacific) and is separately weighing (not yet committed) up to
   ~$6.3–7B for a 500MW AI data center in Akita, Japan — filed as a `candidate` since several reports say
   the ticket could route through MGX rather than Mubadala directly, and no financing terms exist yet.

4. **US Government was the most prolific allocator this week**, via two channels: (a) Commerce/NIST's
   coordinated $874M CHIPS Act R&D batch across 7 companies (GlobalFoundries $300M, Kepler $245M,
   Multibeam $140M, Extropic $75M, Thintronics $50M, OBSIDIA $34M, Aeluma $30M — announced Jul 29, each
   taking a minority non-voting equity stake), and (b) DoD/Department of War's Office of Strategic Capital
   signing $2.03B in critical-minerals/battery deals on Aug 7 (Sila Nanotechnologies $1.4B loan, Sunrise
   Energy Metals $400M loan, Niron Magnetics $150M loan, Strategic Bauxite USA $85.5M equity) — all filed
   under `defense-tech`/`critical-materials` as the closest canonical fit for national-security supply-chain
   funding rather than direct AI compute.

## Biggest signals
- Sovereigns keep paying for AI infrastructure with **follow-on capital into positions they already
  control** (MGX/Aligned) rather than only fresh entries — a maturing pattern, not just deal-sourcing.
- The US Government's CHIPS-for-equity structure (minority, non-voting stakes as a funding condition) is
  now a repeatable instrument, not a one-off — this is the second such batch after the May 2026 quantum-
  computing round, and DoD's OSC is running the same equity-for-capital playbook on the critical-minerals
  side.
- No headline sovereign "mega-fund target" announcements this week (no new $X00B AI-fund pledges) — the
  activity was concrete, named-target capital rather than round-number targets, so amount_estimated=1 was
  used sparingly (only the still-unconfirmed Mubadala Japan lead).
