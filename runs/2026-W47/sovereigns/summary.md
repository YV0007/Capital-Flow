## Biggest signals
1. **Infra (Saudi National Infrastructure Fund) → HUMAIN, $667M project finance (Nov 8).**
   Two financing facilities — SAR1.1bn (~$293.3M) for GPU procurement and SAR1.4bn
   (~$373.3M) for power/grid infrastructure — signed in Riyadh, the first transactions
   under Infra's new "AI Data Center Financing Program." This is a NEW allocator for
   the watchlist: Infra is a distinct Saudi government entity from PIF (under the
   National Development Fund), even though HUMAIN is PIF-owned and PIF's governor
   attended the signing. Filed under Infra, not PIF, and added to
   discovered_allocators.csv. The power facility is sized for up to 1GW of future IT
   load against only ~25MW of GPUs actually being financed now — a clear case of
   Saudi Arabia pre-provisioning grid capacity years ahead of compute, worth watching
   for the next tranche.
2. **DOE Genesis Mission Phase II, $159M across 12 projects (Oct 8).** Tier-1
   (energy.gov) confirmed grant tranche for "Super Intelligence for science" — AI
   R&D awards to national labs and universities (Argonne, Oak Ridge, Fermilab, MIT,
   Harvard, Commonwealth Fusion Systems, etc.), capping a 297-project first-year
   cohort. Non-dilutive government AI money, exactly the US Government class gotcha
   (don't skip it just because it's not an equity check).
3. **Context, not filed:** Aramco's $2.6B cash-plus-assets purchase of 49% of HUMAIN
   (closed Nov 1) is real and large, but the capital-deploying party is Aramco
   (corporate), not PIF — PIF is diluting, not allocating. Left for the corporate
   agent; noted here only as background shaping Saudi Arabia's AI-financing picture
   this month.
