## Biggest signals

1. **Brookfield + Drax (UK), announced 2026-11-03** — Brookfield (via its "Radiant" AI
   data-center platform, funded through the Brookfield-QIA "Qai" $20B JV) agreed to
   develop gigawatt-scale AI data center capacity at Drax Power Station, North Yorkshire.
   Brookfield pegs total potential investment at over £20bn (~$26.8B) across three
   phases — an explicit program estimate, not a closed amount. Phase 1 alone (~£2.5bn /
   ~$3.35B, up to 100MW, targeting COD Q1 2028) is still contingent on planning approvals,
   anchor tenants and a final investment decision. Source: Drax's own RNS filing (Tier 1).

2. **Brookfield + NAVER (South Korea), filed 2026-10-27** — NAVER's regulatory filing
   confirmed Brookfield will fund a wholly-owned SPV ("Asset Co") with up to $9B to buy
   GPUs and build data-center infrastructure for NAVER's GAK Sejong AI-factory expansion
   (55MW → 200MW), unlocking Nvidia's parallel $1B equity investment in NAVER. This
   converts the nonbinding July 2026 term sheet into a signed financing agreement.
   Graded verified_alpha on two independently-opened press accounts (Korea Herald,
   Baxtel/KED Global); the underlying NAVER DART filing itself could not be opened
   directly this run.

3. **Apollo/Goldman Sachs SpaceX $40B GPU lease (2026-10-26)** — confirmed still on file
   from prior runs via `engine.edgar exists`; not re-filed. Notable for widening the
   alt-manager bench: BlackRock and KKR-advised capital accounts both made undisclosed
   anchor commitments alongside Apollo and Goldman, meaning four of our nine tracked
   alt-managers now touch a single mega-financing.
