## Biggest signals
1. **Sequoia's ~$10B refit (fund_launch, verified_alpha).** On 2026-11-04
   Sequoia posted an internal memo ("The Sequoia of Tomorrow") announcing it
   has raised roughly $10B in new funds under new co-stewards Alfred Lin and
   Pat Grady, while simultaneously closing its London office and ending the
   Arc and Scouts programs. No per-vintage breakdown or AI-specific earmark
   was disclosed. This is the single largest capital event on file for the
   firm this year and signals a structural bet on concentrated, late-stage AI
   and "reindustrialization" deployment over breadth.
2. **Sequoia leads Trajectory's $45M Series A (verified_alpha).** A genuine
   new-money, newly-disclosed deal (2026-11-02) for an 18-person post-training
   / continual-learning startup, with Nvidia, Bessemer, and Bain Capital
   Ventures joining. Notable for Nvidia's strategic participation alongside a
   pure financial lead.
3. **Hex's $100M Series D pulls in both Sequoia and a16z as participants
   (verified, Tier 1).** Peak XV and 137 Ventures led; Sequoia and a16z both
   joined as non-lead participants alongside Amplify, Avra, Next Legacy,
   Redpoint, and Zero Prime. Filed as one row per allocator per the
   sector_swarm dedup rule, both with blank amount_usd and
   round_total_usd=$100M since per-allocator slices weren't disclosed.
