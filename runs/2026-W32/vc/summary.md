## What moved this week

The elite-VC money this window clustered in **two canonical sectors**, both away
from the usual frontier-model labs:

1. **defense-tech (the loudest signal).** Two priced rounds, five watchlist-VC
   participations between them.
   - **Senra Systems — $65M Series B (2026-07-15).** Co-led by Lowercarbon +
     Interlagos, but **Sequoia, a16z AND Founders Fund all participate** — three
     watchlist names in one aerospace/defense manufacturing (wire-harness) round.
     Confirmed by Senra's official PRNewswire release (Tier 1).
   - **Cathedral — ~$160M at $1.4B (disclosed 2026-07-22).** **a16z + Sequoia
     co-lead**, both take board seats, in a stealth AI military-cyber startup
     founded by four ex-DOGE staffers. verified_alpha — multiple Tier-3 press
     agree, company in stealth so amount is press-reported (estimated).
   - Net: **defense-tech shows a 3-distinct-investor swarm** (Sequoia, a16z,
     Founders Fund across the two deals) — the strongest cross-VC signal of the week.

2. **ai-compute (custom silicon).** **Etched — $300M Series C at $10.3B
   (2026-07-23), Sequoia lead + a16z participant.** Etched's own PR calls it the
   highest valuation ever for a Sequoia-led Series C. Transformer-specialized
   inference ASIC — a direct AI-buildout hardware bet. Tier-1 confirmed.

3. **robotics.** **Atoms — $1.7B, a16z lead (2026-07-22).** Travis Kalanick's
   industrial-AI/robotics rollup (CloudKitchens + Pronto → Food/Mining/Transport);
   Ben Horowitz joins the board, Uber re-invests. Largest single VC-led check of
   the week. verified_alpha (multiple Tier-3 + Dealroom; no official a16z PR found).
