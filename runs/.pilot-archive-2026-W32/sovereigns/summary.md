# Sovereigns — ISO Week 2026-W32 (window ~2026-07-06 to 2026-08-06)

## Scoreboard
- Verified / verified_alpha events: 4 (2 verified Tier-1, 2 verified_alpha Tier-3)
- Candidate events: 3

## What moved this week
Two distinct engines drove sovereign/state capital into the AI stack: the US
Government via loan-and-grant industrial policy, and Abu Dhabi's MGX via a record
fund close plus a data-center growth commitment. Saudi PIF and Mubadala were loud
but mostly in non-binding MoUs and portfolio pledges — headline pledges, not
committed deals.

## Biggest signals
1. **MGX closes Fund I at $49bn (disclosed Jul 1).** Above its $45bn target; one of
   the largest AI-dedicated funds ever, deploying across semiconductors, AI infra
   and platforms (~$10bn/yr planned). verified_alpha (Tier-3 press + MGX IR; Tier-1
   page not fetchable). Disclosed Jul 1 — just outside a strict 30-day window but too
   central to omit.
2. **US DOE closes up to $3.26bn loan to AEP Texas (Jul 8).** Energy Dominance
   Financing; ~100 grid/transmission projects, explicitly enabling up to 41 GW of new
   AI-data-center and advanced-manufacturing load. Tier-1 (energy.gov) verified. This
   is the clearest "state money → power for AI" flow of the week.
3. **MGX + BlackRock GIP + AIP commit $5bn growth capital to Aligned Data Centers
   (Jul 21)** after closing the ~$40bn buyout. Real committed follow-on into named
   hyperscale capacity; MGX's individual share undisclosed. verified_alpha.
4. **CHIPS grants (Jul 13 / Jul 29):** Bosch $225m Direct Funding Agreement (signed,
   verified) for a SiC fab; plus $874m of letters of intent to 7 compute-supply-chain
   companies (GlobalFoundries $300m, Kepler $245m, etc.) with the government taking
   minority equity — recorded as candidates because they are LOIs, not final awards.
