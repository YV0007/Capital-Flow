# VC capital allocation — week 2026-W45

## What moved
Watchlist VC activity in the trailing ~30 days was thin on brand-new deals (most big-ticket
items — Sequoia's $7B AI fund, Founders Fund's $6B fund, Khosla's $5.5B fundraise, Thrive's
$10B fund, Anthropic's $65B/$30B rounds — were already reported in prior weeks or are still
"in talks," not closed). Four fresh, dated events cleared the bar this week:

1. **Serval (Sequoia-led, $150M Series C @ $3B, Oct 29)** — enterprise AI agents gunning for
   ServiceNow's turf. Sequoia's third check into the company in 12 months (seed->B->C), a
   clean signal of conviction-stacking in enterprise-ai-agents.
2. **Kalshi ($1B Series G @ $40B, Oct 29, co-led by Sequoia + Wellington Management, a16z
   participating)** — valuation nearly doubled from $22B in May. Not an AI-buildout company
   (it's a CFTC-regulated prediction market), but two key allocators (Sequoia, a16z) both
   deepened an existing position in the same round — worth tracking as a capital-flow event
   even though it sits outside the canonical AI sectors.
3. **General Intuition (Khosla participating, $220M @ $6.2B, early Oct)** — world/action
   models trained on gameplay video (spun out of Medal). Valuation nearly tripled from $2.3B
   in June. Khosla has backed every round (seed, Series A, this one) but did not lead this
   time — Valor Equity Partners stepped in as lead investor.

## Biggest signals
- **Enterprise-AI-agents is where Sequoia keeps doubling down** — Serval is the third Sequoia-
  led enterprise-agent name to resurface in recent weeks (alongside Rogo, Pace — both already
  on file from earlier runs), and valuations are compounding fast (Serval 1B -> 3B in under a
  year).
- **Valuation inflation outpacing new capital** — General Intuition and Kalshi both show
  valuations roughly tripling/doubling in a few months on rounds where the *existing*
  watchlist investor (Khosla, Sequoia/a16z) didn't necessarily lead the new money — a sign of
  secondary/insider marks as much as fresh conviction.
- **No fresh watchlist fund closes this week.** Khosla's reported $5.5B raise is still "in
  talks" per the only sourcing found (July 2026); Coatue's early-stage vehicle and Databricks
  round (Coatue-led, ~$3B) were mid-summer events, outside this window.

## What to watch next week
- Whether Khosla's $5.5B multi-fund raise (seed/early/opportunity) actually closes — would be
  a `fund_launch` event once confirmed by a primary source.
- Databricks' Coatue-led ~$3B round: press said it signed a term sheet July 16 and expected to
  close "later in summer" — worth a forward EDGAR/Form D check to see if it ever formally

- Anthropic's IPO-track S-1 filing (CNBC reported Oct 26) could resolve existing Sequoia/

  a Tier-1 source.
- General Intuition's date is split across sources (Sept 29-30 vs Oct 5) — worth re-confirming
  against a primary filing/announcement next week.
