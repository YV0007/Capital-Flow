# Sovereigns — Week 2026-W45

## What moved

sovereign/government capital into the physical AI-power stack and a routine PIF->Lucid
drawdown. Three verified rows, all Tier-1 (SEC filing or official government/company PR),

## Top signals
1. **US Government -> Constellation Energy, up to $5.0B (conditional DOE loan, 2026-10-27).**
   DOE's Office of Energy Dominance Financing committed to finance nuclear uprates at 8
   Constellation plants (~1.1GW). Not yet a done deal — subject to due diligence — but the
   cleanest AI-power-demand story of the week: Constellation's own release ties most of the
   new capacity to 20-year PPAs already signed with Google and Amazon, plus a smaller Meta
   deal. This is the clearest "capital following AI load growth" signal on file this run.
2. **US Government -> Alliant Energy, $5.1B (closed DOE loan, 2026-10-26/28).** A day after
   the Constellation announcement, DOE closed (not just committed — loan agreements executed)
   a $5.1B, 30-year loan to Alliant's Iowa and Wisconsin utilities for ~1.6GW new gas
   generation, ~1GW battery storage and LNG storage. Unlike Constellation's, neither DOE's nor
   Alliant's release explicitly frames this as AI-driven — filed as power-energy / project_finance
   with the AI-tie flagged as tentative rather than tagged with the `energy_for_ai` theme.
3. **Saudi PIF -> Lucid Group, $400M (SEC 8-K, drawn 2026-10-06).** Routine but real: another
   tranche drawn under the Ayar Third Investment Company (PIF affiliate) delayed-draw term
   loan, the fourth 2026 draw on this facility (April $500M, July $800M — already on file —,
   August $400M, now October $400M). ~$400M of the $2.5B facility remains undrawn.
